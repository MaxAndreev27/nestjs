# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Giving and accepting feedback with an open mind.
- Focusing on the issue, not the person.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and technical approaches.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and helping to correct them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, threats, doxxing, sexualized
comments, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.
Disruptive behavior in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces
may also be addressed by the maintainers.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may remove or edit comments, close issues or pull requests, or take other
actions they consider appropriate and proportionate to the situation.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the private
contact options on the repository owner's GitHub profile:

<https://github.com/MaxAndreev27>

Please include a description of what happened, relevant links or screenshots, and
any context needed to investigate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other channels used for project purposes.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code-of-conduct.html>
